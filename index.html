About Me

Driven by structure,
fueled by curiosity.

Hi, I'm Rindang — a third-year Informatics student at Politeknik Elektronika Negeri Surabaya (PENS), passionate about the intersection of technology, people, and process.

As Project Manager at e-code, I lead cross-functional teams to plan and ship technology projects within the campus ecosystem. I believe great project management is about more than timelines — it's about creating clarity and momentum for every person involved.

Outside of tech, I'm an Scrum Master at BudgEx and functionaries of UKM Dirgantara and have experience as a Master of Ceremony at PKKMB x Technogear 2025, where I helped set the tone for hundreds of new students entering the world of tech education.
